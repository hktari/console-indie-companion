# Console Indie Companion

A voice companion mobile/web app for console players of single-player indie games.

## The Problem

Console platforms (PS5, Xbox, Switch) lack the overlay ecosystem PC gamers enjoy:
- No Discord overlay for co-op vibes while playing solo
- No easy access to spoiler-free hints
- No way to discuss lore/story in real-time
- No progress tracking without breaking immersion

Questie.ai and similar solutions require PC screen capture — console players are left out.

## The Solution

A **mobile companion app** that sits next to you while you play console indie games:

- **Voice chat** — hands-free while playing
- **Game-specific knowledge** — not generic AI, trained on specific indie titles
- **Memory** — remembers your progress, preferences, past discussions
- **Graduated hints** — helps without spoiling
- **Lore/story discussion** — like having a friend who's played it before
- **Progress tracking** — what you've found, what's next

### Vertical Examples
- **Hollow Knight Companion** — boss strategies, charm builds, secret locations
- **Stardew Valley Companion** — crop planners, friendship trackers, daily guides
- **Hades Companion** — build discussions, mirror recommendations, lore chat
- **Celeste Companion** — emotional support, strawberry hunting, chapter guides

## Target Customers

### B2C: End Users
- Console players of single-player indie games
- Subscription model: $5-10/month
- Early adopters: PS5/Xbox Game Pass indie players

### B2B: Indie Game Studios
- White-label companion apps for their games
- Licensing/SDK model
- Value prop: higher engagement, longer play sessions, new revenue stream

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Platform | Focus | Gap We Fill |
|------------|----------|-------|-------------|
| Questie.ai | PC | Screen capture, hints + lore | **No console support** |
| Gaming Copilot | PC | Voice help, walkthroughs | **No console support** |
| Character.AI | Mobile | General roleplay | **Not game-specific** |
| HakkoAI | PC | Multi-genre gaming | **Not indie-focused** |

**Our differentiation:** Console-first + indie-vertical + mobile companion
