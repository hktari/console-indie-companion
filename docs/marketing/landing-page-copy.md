## FAQ

**Q: Does this work on all consoles?**
> A: Yes! PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch, and even PC. It's a mobile app, so it works alongside any platform.

**Q: Will it spoil my game?**
> A: Never, unless you explicitly ask. The companion is trained to give graduated hints and track what you've already discovered.

**Q: Do I need to pause my game to use it?**
> A: Nope! Voice mode is hands-free. Keep playing while you chat.

**Q: What games are supported?**
> A: We're starting with Hollow Knight, Stardew Valley, Hades, and Celeste. Vote for the next game when you join the waitlist.
