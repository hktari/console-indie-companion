# Competitive Analysis

## Direct Competitors

### Questie.ai
**Website:** https://www.questie.ai  
**Platform:** PC (Windows/Mac/Linux)  
**Funding/Status:** Active, appears bootstrapped/seed

**What They Do:**
- AI companion that watches your screen via capture
- Voice chat banter like a Discord buddy
- Real-time gameplay reaction
- Custom characters with personalities
- Works with fantasy, anime, and realistic themes

**Key Features:**
- Screen capture integration
- Vision-enabled AI (sees your gameplay)
- Voice conversation
- Character customization
- Supports major LLMs (GPT, Claude, Gemini)

**Pricing:**
- Freemium model suspected
- Premium features likely subscription-based

**Strengths:**
- First-mover in AI gaming companion space
- Strong PC integration
- Active community on Reddit
- Good UX from videos/screenshots

**Weaknesses (Our Opportunities):**
- **No console support** — PC only
- Requires screen capture (privacy concerns for some)
- Generic across games (not indie-vertical)
- No memory/continuity between sessions

**Key Insight:**
Their "Game Walkthroughs" page focuses on graduated hints and spoiler-free help — exactly the problem space we're targeting, but for PC only.

## Adjacent/Indirect Competitors

### Character.AI, Poly.AI, Moemate, Janitor AI
**What They Do:** General AI roleplay/chat platforms

**Relevance:** Gamers use these for game-related roleplay, but:
- Not game-aware (no gameplay context)
- Not spoiler-aware
- No progress tracking
- Generic personalities

**Opportunity:** Pull gamers from generic roleplay to game-specific companions.

---

### Traditional Walkthrough Sites
**Examples:** GameFAQs, IGN Walkthroughs, Fextralife Wikis

**Strengths:**
- Comprehensive information
- Free
- Established trust

**Weaknesses:**
- All-or-nothing (spoil everything)
- No interactivity
- Static, not adaptive
- No companion experience

**Opportunity:** Position as "the anti-walkthrough" — graduated, interactive, companion-like.

---

### Discord Communities
**Examples:** Game-specific Discord servers

**Strengths:**
- Live community
- Real humans
- Social connection

**Weaknesses:**
- Not always online
- Spoilers are uncontrolled
- No game-aware help
- Toxicity in some communities

**Opportunity:** Provide 24/7, spoiler-aware, always-positive companion.

## Strategic Positioning

### Against Questie.ai
**Questie:** PC screen capture, generic companion  
**Us:** Console mobile app, indie-vertical, game-specific knowledge

**Messaging:**
> "Questie is great for PC, but console players deserve companions too. We're building the first companion app designed for PS5, Xbox, and Switch indie games."

### Against Traditional Walkthroughs
**Walkthroughs:** All-or-nothing information dumps  
**Us:** Graduated, conversational, companion experience

**Messaging:**
> "Walkthroughs spoil everything. Wikis are boring. We're the friend who's beaten it before — there when you need a nudge, quiet when you don't."

### Against Discord
**Discord:** Sometimes online, uncontrolled spoilers  
**Us:** Always on, spoiler-aware, positive companion

**Messaging:**
> "Your Discord friends aren't always online, and they might spoil the ending. We're always there, never spoil unless you ask, and actually know the game."

## Competitive Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Questie adds console support | Medium | Move fast, build indie relationships first |
| Big player (Razer, Discord) enters | Low-Medium | Indie-vertical moat, studio partnerships |
| Free alternatives dominate | Low | Premium features (memory, deep knowledge) worth paying for |
| Studios build their own | Medium | White-label makes us the easy choice |

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Console gap is real** — None of the major players support console properly
2. **Indie-vertical is open** — Everyone is going broad, not deep per-game
3. **Memory/continuity missing** — Most solutions are session-only
4. **Spoiler-awareness is hard** — Requires game-specific training
5. **B2B opportunity exists** — Studios need this but don't want to build it
