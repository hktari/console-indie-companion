# Studio Outreach Templates

## Template 1: Initial Cold Email

**Subject:** Quick question about [Game Name] player support

```
Hi [Name],

I just played [Game Name] and loved it — especially [specific detail]. 
But I got stuck on [specific part] and had to Google it, which spoiled the next section.

Made me wonder: how do you currently help players who get stuck? Do you track where people drop off?

I'm building an AI companion app for indie games on console (since there's no overlay support like PC). 
The idea is graduated hints without spoilers, lore discussion, and progress tracking — like having a friend who's beaten it before.

**Not selling anything right now** — just researching whether studios would find this valuable.

Quick question: Would something like this help [Game Name] players? Or is your current support system working well?

Either way, appreciate your time and the amazing game.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Template 2: Response to Positive Reply

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about [Game Name] player support

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the quick reply! Glad to hear it resonates.

To give you a clearer picture:

**What the companion does:**
- Players open the mobile app while playing on console
- Voice or text chat for hints, lore discussion, "what should I do next?"
- Game-specific knowledge (not generic AI)
- Remembers their progress across sessions
- Never spoils unless they explicitly ask

**Benefits for you:**
- Higher completion rates (players don't quit when stuck)
- Longer average session time
- Better reviews (less frustration)
- Optional: revenue share on subscriptions

**What we need from you:**
- Access to game knowledge (wiki, walkthrough docs, lore bible)
- Feedback on what your players actually need
- Permission for case study (if it works well)

**Pilot terms:**
- Free setup for first 3-5 studios
- Revenue share: [X%] of subscriptions from your game
- You maintain full creative control over tone/content
- White-label option (looks like your branding)

Interested in a 15-minute call to discuss? I'm flexible on timing.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Calendar link]
```

## Response Scenarios

### If they say "we're not interested":
> "Totally understand — may I ask what would make this interesting in the future? Just so I know whether to follow up down the road."

### If they say "we're building our own":
> "That's awesome! Building it yourself gives you full control. Quick question: would you ever consider white-label to save dev time, or is owning the tech core to your strategy?"

### If they say "maybe later":
> "Great — I'll check back in [3 months/after your next release]. Mind if I add you to our beta list so you can see what we build?"

### If they say "tell me more":
→ Send Template 2 immediately, offer calendar link

---

## Best Practices

1. **Lead with value, not ask** — Show you've played their game
2. **Be specific** — Mention actual game moments, not generic praise
3. **Research first** — Check their Discord, Twitter, recent updates
4. **No attachments** — Keep it text-only for email deliverability
5. **Follow up once** — Then let it go
6. **Track everything** — Use the spreadsheet above
