# Interview Questions

## Console Indie Gamer Interviews

### Warm-up (5 minutes)

1. Tell me about your gaming setup. What consoles do you play on?
2. What was the last indie game you played solo?
3. Walk me through that gaming session — what did you do?

### Problem Discovery (10 minutes)

4. When you're playing a solo indie game, do you ever feel like you want company?
   - If yes: Tell me more about that. When does it happen?
   - If no: What do you enjoy about playing alone?

5. Have you ever gotten stuck in a game? What did you do?
   - Walk me through the exact steps
   - What frustrated you about that process?
   - Did you find what you needed? Was it worth it?

6. Do you ever look up guides or wikis while playing?
   - When do you decide to look something up?
   - What do you use? (Google, Reddit, Discord, YouTube, etc.)
   - Have you ever been spoiled by a guide? Tell me about it.

7. Do you use Discord or other chat while playing solo games?
   - What do you use it for?
   - Do you wish you had someone to discuss the game with?

### Solution Validation (10 minutes)

8. Imagine you have a mobile app next to you while playing. You can talk to it like a friend who's played the game before.
   - What would you ask it?
   - When would you use it?
   - When would you NOT want it to interrupt?

9. Which of these features would be most valuable to you? (Rank 1-5)
   - Spoiler-free hints when stuck
   - Lore/story discussion
   - Progress tracking (what you've found, what's next)
   - Game recommendations based on your taste
   - Finding other players to chat with

10. Would you actually talk to an AI through your phone while playing?
    - What would make that feel natural?
    - What would feel awkward?

### Pricing & Willingness to Pay (5 minutes)

11. If this app existed, would you pay for it?
    - If yes: What would feel fair? ($3, $5, $10, $15/month?)
    - If no: What would need to be different?

12. Would you prefer:
    - Subscription ($X/month)
    - One-time purchase ($X)
    - Free with ads
    - Free with limited features

### Closing (5 minutes)

13. What indie games would you want this for first?
14. Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?
15. Can I contact you when we have a beta ready?

## Indie Game Developer Interviews

### Warm-up (5 minutes)

1. Tell me about your game. What's it about?
2. What platforms is it on?
3. Who's your typical player?

### Current Player Support (10 minutes)

4. How do you currently help players who get stuck?
   - In-game hints? External guides? Discord community?
   - What's working? What's not?

5. Do you track where players drop off or quit?
   - What are the common quit points?
   - Why do you think they quit?

6. Do players ever ask for help?
   - Where do they ask? (Discord, email, Reddit, etc.)
   - What do they ask about?
   - How long does it take you to respond?

### Companion App Concept (10 minutes)

7. What if players had an AI companion that:
   - Gave graduated hints when stuck (no spoilers)
   - Discussed lore/story with them
   - Tracked their progress
   - Worked on console (mobile app)
   
   Would that help your players?

8. Would that benefit your studio?
   - Higher completion rates?
   - Longer session times?
   - Better reviews?
   - Anything else?

9. Would you want a companion app specifically for your game?
   - What would it need to do?
   - What would make it feel "on-brand" for your game?

### Business Model (10 minutes)

10. How would you want to work with a companion app provider?
    - White-label (your branding, our tech)?
    - Partnership (co-branded)?
    - Referral (you recommend us)?
    - Something else?

11. What would you pay for a companion app solution?
    - Per-player fee?
    - Monthly subscription?
    - Revenue share?
    - Upfront + rev share?

12. What would make this a "must-have" vs "nice-to-have" for your game?

### Closing (5 minutes)

13. Who else should I talk to?
14. Can I follow up with a demo when ready?

## Interview Logistics

### Finding Interviewees

**Gamers:**
- Reddit DMs from validation posts
- Waitlist signups who opt-in
- Discord communities (ask mods first)
- Friends/friends-of-friends who play indies

**Developers:**
- r/playmygame responses
- Twitter DMs to indie devs
- Indie game Discord servers
- Game conference attendees

### Incentives

**Gamers:**
- $20 Amazon gift card
- OR 3 months free subscription (when launched)
- OR early beta access + name in credits

**Developers:**
- Free companion app for their game
- Revenue share opportunity
- Case study/feature on our blog

### Recording & Notes

- Record video calls (with permission)
- Take detailed notes immediately after
- Tag quotes by theme (problem, solution, pricing, etc.)

## Analysis Framework

After each interview, score:

| Factor | Score (1-5) | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Problem intensity | | How painful is the current experience? |
| Solution appeal | | How excited are they about the concept? |
| Feature priorities | | Which features matter most? |
| Price sensitivity | | What would they actually pay? |
| Use case clarity | | When/how would they use it? |

**Aggregate scores across all interviews to identify patterns.**
